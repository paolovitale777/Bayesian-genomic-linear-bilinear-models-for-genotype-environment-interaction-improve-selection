# Wheat_599

Spring wheat, 599 lines in 4 mega-environments; genomic relationship matrix G.

Trait: grain yield.

Files: `Wheat_599.rds` (read with `load_dataset("Wheat_599")` in `data/load_dataset.R`) and `Wheat_599_pheno.csv` (phenotypes).

Source: Crossa et al. (2010); Cuevas et al. (2025); CIMMYT Research Data & Software Repository Network, https://hdl.handle.net/11529/10549160 (redistributed with the agreement of the data authors).
