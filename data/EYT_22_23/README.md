# EYT_22_23

CIMMYT elite yield trial 2022–23: spring wheat, 1004 lines in 6 management conditions at Ciudad Obregón; 3672 SNPs (G = MM'/m is recomputed when loaded).

Trait: grain yield.

Files: `EYT_22_23.rds` (read with `load_dataset("EYT_22_23")` in `data/load_dataset.R`) and `EYT_22_23_pheno.csv` (phenotypes).

Source: Cuevas et al. (2025); CIMMYT Research Data & Software Repository Network, https://hdl.handle.net/11529/10549160 (redistributed with the agreement of the data authors).
