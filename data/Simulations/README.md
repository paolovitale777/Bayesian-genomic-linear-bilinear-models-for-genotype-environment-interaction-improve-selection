# Simulations

The simulated data sets S1 (bilinear G×E, 4 environments), S2 (bilinear G×E, 8 environments) and S3 (reaction-norm G×E, 4 environments) are not stored: they are generated by `simulate_case()` in `gsreg_functions.R`, with fixed seeds, from the genomic relationship matrix of Wheat 599 (`data/Wheat_599`). See `gsreg_simulation.R` and Section 2.5 of the paper.
