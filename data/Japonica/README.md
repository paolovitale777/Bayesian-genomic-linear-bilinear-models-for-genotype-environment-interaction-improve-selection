# Japonica

Japonica rice, 320 lines (93–316 per year) in 5 years (2009–2013); 16,383 SNPs; 54 environmental covariables per year.

Trait: grain yield.

Files: `Japonica.rds` (read with `load_dataset("Japonica")` in `data/load_dataset.R`) and `Japonica_pheno.csv` (phenotypes).

Source: Monteverde et al. (2019); https://github.com/osval78/New_GE_Framework.
